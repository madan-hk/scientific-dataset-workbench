Tasks, datasets, and sample decisions are saved as real JSON files on your computer -- not in the browser. Pick the RNA-project folder to connect it; the app reads/writes its tasks/ subfolder directly.

Needs Chrome, Edge, or Brave. You may need to do this again each time you reopen the app -- that's a browser security requirement for local files, not a bug.
